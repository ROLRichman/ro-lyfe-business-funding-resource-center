# RO’Lyfe Business Funding & Grant Resource Center

**Repository:** `ROLRichman/ro-lyfe-business-funding-resource-center`  
**Business:** Root Of Lyfe LLC  
**Positioning:** Business funding readiness, grant research, startup resources, and responsible advisor navigation.

## What this project is
A mobile-friendly resource hub for Root Of Lyfe's own funding-readiness work and, over time, support for entrepreneurs and startups through opportunity research, preparation, and referrals to qualified organizations.

## First release
- Responsive single-page resource center in `index.html`
- Filterable directory by funding type, business stage, and geography
- Grant-readiness checklist with progress indicator
- Workflow: Discover → Screen → Prepare → Refer & follow up
- Plain-language grant, loan, advisor, and referral distinctions
- Official resource links and eligibility cautions
- No backend and no collection of client personal data

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. Open repository **Settings → Pages**.
2. Select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose `main` and `/ (root)`.
4. Save and wait for deployment.

## Project roadmap
1. Verify Root Of Lyfe's legal details, business activities, revenue sources, location, licenses, tax standing, and proposed project.
2. Build a structured opportunity tracker with administrator, geography, eligibility, award range, allowable uses, deadlines, source URL, last-verified date, and status.
3. Add a privacy-conscious client intake workflow with explicit consent and secure document handling.
4. Build an advisor/partner directory with scope, credentials, fees, conflicts, referral terms, and verification date.
5. Prepare reusable business narrative, project budget, projections, outcomes, and supporting-document checklist.
6. Add authenticated backend storage only when necessary, with least-privilege access, retention rules, and audit trail.

## Guardrails
- Root Of Lyfe is not a grant-making agency, government agency, lender, law firm, or tax advisor.
- Never guarantee grants, approvals, or investment returns.
- Program administrators make final eligibility and award decisions.
- Do not represent a loan as a grant or a referral as approval.
- Avoid percentage-of-award compensation and promises that payment secures funding.
- Use a written, transparent scope and fee; disclose referral compensation and conflicts.
- Confirm applicable licensing, consumer-protection, privacy, and program-specific rules before offering services.
- Verify each opportunity with its official administrator before recommending an application.
- Never place EIN letters, tax returns, bank statements, SSNs, identity documents, passwords, or client records in this public repository.

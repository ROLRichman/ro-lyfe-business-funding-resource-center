# Grant Advisor & Funding Readiness Playbook

## Purpose
Prepare Root Of Lyfe LLC for a professional grant-readiness consultation and establish a repeatable, ethical process for helping other entrepreneurs navigate funding resources.

## A. Root Of Lyfe's private readiness file
Assemble these materials privately. Do not upload sensitive records to this public GitHub repository.

- PA formation / good-standing documents and current legal entity name.
- EIN confirmation letter.
- Current business address and proof of location if required.
- Business licenses, registrations, and tax account status.
- Last two years of business tax returns, if available and requested.
- Year-to-date profit and loss, balance sheet, revenue, expenses, debt payments, and employee count.
- Business bank statements only when requested through a verified secure channel.
- Description of actual revenue-generating activities and percentage of revenue by activity.
- Project description: what will be built, who it serves, timeline, itemized cost, and measurable outcomes.
- Vendor estimates or quotes for technology, equipment, marketing, or infrastructure.
- 24-month projections with assumptions.

## B. Root Of Lyfe project narrative — working draft

**Business overview**  
Root Of Lyfe LLC is a Pennsylvania business developing a business resource and opportunity-navigation platform. Its work includes business funding resource education, real-estate funding referral activity, business-development resources, affiliate and partner relationships, and digital systems that organize information and workflows.

**Project concept**  
Develop and improve a digital Business Funding & Grant Resource Center that helps the company organize verified funding resources, prepare its own business growth plan, and provide entrepreneurs with clearer pathways to technical assistance and qualified funding organizations.

**Potential project investments to validate**
- Website and secure digital infrastructure.
- Research and opportunity-tracking tools.
- Client workflow and document-management systems with appropriate security.
- Business planning, professional consulting, and compliance support.
- Marketing and outreach tied to measurable customer acquisition.

**Potential outcomes to measure**
- Number of verified funding programs tracked.
- Number of businesses completing a readiness review.
- Number of referrals to qualified technical-assistance providers.
- Number of clients completing a business plan or funding package.
- New customers, revenue growth, capacity improvements, and jobs created where applicable.

This is a draft concept, not a claim that these expenses are grant-eligible. Each program has its own allowable-use rules. Tie the final request to a real, priced project and measurable growth case.

## E. Future client service model
1. **Discovery:** understand the business, stage, location, project, and funding purpose.
2. **Basic eligibility screen:** compare verified facts with published requirements. Mark unknown items as unknown; never infer eligibility.
3. **Readiness plan:** identify missing documents, projections, project budget, measurable outcomes, and technical-assistance needs.
4. **Referral:** refer to the official administrator, SBDC, CDFI, lender, or qualified professional. Disclose relationships and compensation.
5. **Application support:** only offer drafting or submission assistance under a written scope, clear fee, privacy terms, and client authorization.
6. **Follow-up:** track deadlines, submitted materials, administrator questions, and final outcome.

## F. Payment and ethics policy
- Offer a clearly defined no-cost discovery conversation where feasible.
- Do not charge for access to a government grant or promise approval.
- Do not take a percentage of a grant award or use a success-fee promise.
- If paid support is offered, use a fixed project fee or hourly rate with deliverables and payment milestones stated in advance.
- Disclose referral relationships and conflicts.
- Confirm legal, regulatory, privacy, and program-specific requirements before accepting client work.
- Never ask clients to send passwords, banking logins, SSNs, or unredacted identity documents through email or an unsecured form.

## G. Catalyst Fund caution
The Philadelphia Small Business Catalyst Fund publishes eligibility requirements and exclusions. Its FAQ excludes certain financial institutions primarily engaged in lending, passive real-estate companies/investors, and businesses earning most revenue from rentals. Root Of Lyfe should not assume eligibility or ineligibility solely from the phrase "funding broker." Ask the City or SBDC to review actual activities, revenue mix, location, tax status, and the proposed project.

Official references:
- https://www.phila.gov/programs/the-catalyst-fund/
- https://www.phila.gov/programs/the-catalyst-fund/frequently-asked-questions/

## H. Review cadence
For each opportunity, record:
- Last verified date
- Application window and deadline
- Eligibility source
- Award type and amount
- Match requirement, if any
- Eligible and prohibited expenses
- Administrator contact
- Status: researching / advisor review / open / closed / waitlist / archived
